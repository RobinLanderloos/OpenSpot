# Open spots are a template minus role counts

A listing reports how many members have each role (tank/healer/damage) but not the composition the leader wants. OpenSpot therefore computes open spots as *template minus members with that role*: 5-man dungeons use 1 tank / 1 healer / 3 DPS (editable in settings); members without a role count as DPS; activities without a template (raids, other) count as having an open spot for every role while the listing is not full. Listings of single players (not groups) are hidden while a role filter is active, because the filter is for finding a group to join. A "player finder" for building your own group is planned for v2.

## Consequences

The template is a guess. A leader running 2 healers or no healer will be judged against 1/1/3 unless the user edits the template.
